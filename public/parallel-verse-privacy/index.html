Parallel Verse Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Overview

Parallel Verse is a scripture reading app designed to help users read scripture in two languages at once. The app does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information.

Information We Collect

Parallel Verse does not collect, sell, or share personal information through the app.

  • No account registration is required.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers are used by this privacy policy page.
  • Bookmarks, theme choices, and reading preferences are intended to remain on your device.

Support Emails

If you contact support by email, your email address and the contents of your message will be used only to respond to your question, bug report, or feature request.

Children's Privacy

Parallel Verse does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support request, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
